AI Venture Studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

AI Venture Studio is a one-person studio that builds free Mac apps with AI agents and publishes them on this site.

The Mac apps

The apps run entirely on your Mac. They have no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and they do not send what you put into them anywhere. Some apps check GitHub for a newer version, at most once a day; that request goes to GitHub and carries nothing about you beyond what any web request does. "Send Feedback", where an app has it, only opens a GitHub page in your browser when you click it, and nothing is sent unless you submit that page yourself.

Venture Studio Uploader

The studio uses a small tool of its own to post the studio's own short videos to the studio's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Threads, TikTok and Mastodon, through each platform's official API. It acts only for the account owner who authorised it, keeps the access tokens only on the owner's computer, asks only for the permissions needed to upload videos and to confirm which account it is posting to, and does not collect, store or share any information about anyone else.
