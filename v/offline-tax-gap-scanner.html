The opportunity

A local-only macOS desktop app for freelancers and microbusinesses that scans receipts, bank PDFs, and CSV exports to detect missing expense records, categorize deductible items, and surface tax-season gaps before they become expensive problems.

Objective for this build

Build the smallest native macOS SwiftUI prototype that lets a user drop in a folder of sample PDFs and CSVs, lists detected receipts and gaps, and exports a simple summary report; verify it against bundled sample files.

Market and user research

**MARKET SIZE**

Plausible buyers are Mac-using freelancers, sole proprietors, microagencies, and a smaller set of bookkeepers who clean up client books before tax filing.

Bottom-up estimate, US first:
- ESTIMATE 12M-16M people/very small businesses are in the self-employed / microbusiness bucket.
- ESTIMATE 30%-40% use Macs in a workflow where a desktop app is realistic.
- ESTIMATE 35%-50% have messy enough receipt, bank, and CSV handling to feel a real pain.
- ESTIMATE 30%-50% would prefer a local-only tool for privacy, speed, or simplicity.

Arithmetic:
- 12M to 16M x 30% to 40% = ESTIMATE 3.6M to 6.4M Mac-viable prospects.
- 3.6M to 6.4M x 35% to 50% = ESTIMATE 1.3M to 3.2M people with the right pain.
- 1.3M to 3.2M x 30% to 50% willingness to prefer local-only and pay = ESTIMATE 400k to 1.6M plausible buyers.

My base-case estimate is ESTIMATE 700k to 1.0M likely direct buyers in the US, with ESTIMATE 200k to 400k as an initial addressable segment if you focus on Mac-using freelancers who already export bank data and use tax software.
