The opportunity

Receipt Sentinel: a native macOS app for freelancers, contractors, and very small businesses who are drowning in paper and PDF receipts. It solves the painful problem of manual expense sorting, missing receipt detection, and accountant-ready export by doing everything locally on the Mac, with no cloud account and no external service dependency.

Objective for this build

Build a minimal macOS prototype that accepts a folder of sample receipts, extracts text locally, classifies each item into a few expense categories, flags duplicates and missing metadata, and exports a CSV plus a short audit report.
