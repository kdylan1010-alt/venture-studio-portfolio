The opportunity

Vendor 1099 Threshold Copilot: a native macOS desktop app for SMB owners, bookkeepers, and accounting firms that imports bank CSVs, invoice PDFs, and vendor lists; normalizes payee identities; detects missing W-9s; flags payments crossing the 2026 1099-NEC threshold; and exports a filing-prep queue and exception list. The painful problem is year-end 1099 prep spread across messy files and inconsistent vendor records. The value proposition is fewer missed forms, less scrambling, and a clearer filing packet without requiring cloud accounting integrations.

Objective for this build

Build a one-window native macOS app that imports local sample CSV and PDF files, extracts vendor names and year-to-date totals, detects which vendors cross the 2026 1099-NEC threshold, shows a prioritized exception list, and exports a 1099-prep CSV so the thesis can be tested with real messy inputs.
