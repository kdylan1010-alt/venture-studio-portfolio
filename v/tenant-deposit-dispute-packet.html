The opportunity

A local macOS app for renters moving out that turns move-in photos, receipts, inspection notes, and landlord correspondence into a timestamped security-deposit dispute packet. Target user: tenants facing withheld deposits. Painful problem: the evidence is scattered and hard to organize under deadline. Value proposition: an offline, Swift-only desktop app that assembles a clear packet and checklist fast enough to increase the odds of recovery.

Objective for this build

Implement the smallest offline Swift-only prototype for the selected thesis: a single-file macOS app or core that ingests a folder of move-in/move-out evidence, emits a packet summary with pass/fail missing-evidence checks, and verifies one clean fixture plus one crafted fixture.

Market and user research

Implemented a minimal offline Swift prototype at [TenantDepositPacket.swift](/Users/a1111/ai-gateway/venture-prototype/TenantDepositPacket.swift).

What it does:
- Scans a folder recursively for evidence files.
- Classifies files into move-in photo, move-out photo, receipt, landlord correspondence, and inspection note.
- Extracts a date from filename prefixes like `YYYY-MM-DD_...`.
- Emits a packet report with:
  - PASS/FAIL status
  - file count
  - date span
  - evidence checklist
  - missing items
  - sorted timeline

Verification:

- Crafted fixture: FAIL, with missing `receipt` and `landlord correspondence`.
- Self-test ended with `SELF-TEST OK`.
