The opportunity

Procurement Pulse for small contractors, estimators, and boutique firms that lose revenue when they miss bid opportunities or deadline changes. Painful problem: opportunities are scattered across many public portals, updated inconsistently, and easy to miss. Value proposition: a native macOS app that continuously monitors watchlisted procurement sources, filters and deduplicates live notices, flags changed deadlines/status, and exports a prioritized bid queue the user can act on.

Objective for this build

Build a native macOS procurement-monitoring app that lets the user manage watchlists, fetch live notices from public procurement sources, detect new and changed opportunities, surface visible errors and progress, persist settings with UserDefaults, set the app icon via NSApp.applicationIconImage, show an empty state and a window title, include LSMinimumSystemVersion and NSHumanReadableCopyright in Info.plist, export a shortlist, and ship a verifiable .dmg with a README.md.

Market and user research

All market numbers below are inference and `ESTIMATE`, not checked sources.

**MARKET SIZE**
- Who could plausibly buy this: small contractors, estimators, bid/proposal coordinators, and boutique firms that regularly chase public-sector work and lose revenue when they miss a notice or deadline change.
- Best-fit buyers are likely firms in construction, specialty trades, facilities, civil work, small IT/services consultancies, and other small businesses that bid repeatedly but do not have a full bid-intelligence stack.

Bottom-up estimate:
- `ESTIMATE` about `150,000` US firms plausibly do some recurring public-bid hunting or monitor multiple procurement sources.
- `ESTIMATE` about `30%` of those have enough recurring bid volume and pain to care about a dedicated tool: `45,000`.
- `ESTIMATE` about `20%` to `30%` of that group would buy a focused macOS monitor within a couple of years: `9,000` to `13,500` near-term buyers.

Arithmetic:
- `ESTIMATE` `150,000 x 30% x 25% = 11,250` plausible buyers.
- `ESTIMATE` at `$/year` spend of `1,200` per buyer, that is about `11,250 x $1,200 = $13.5M ARR` reachable in a solid niche case.
- Inference: this is a real niche market, but not obviously a huge standalone venture unless it expands into team workflows, source coverage, or adjacent bid-management features.
