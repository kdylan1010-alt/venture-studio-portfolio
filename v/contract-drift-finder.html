The opportunity

A native macOS app for SMB owners, ops managers, and freelancers that opens PDFs locally, extracts contract and invoice text, flags auto-renewals, escalators, duplicate charges, and missing caps, and exports a one-page risk report that shows where money may be leaking.

Objective for this build

Build the smallest usable macOS app that opens a PDF contract or invoice, extracts text locally, detects a short list of risky clause patterns with simple heuristics, and exports a one-page findings report; package that artifact into a local .dmg for testing.
