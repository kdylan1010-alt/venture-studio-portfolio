The opportunity

eInvoice Workbench: a native macOS desktop app for SMEs, bookkeepers, and cross-border sellers that ingests invoice folders, extracts and normalizes fields from PDFs/XML/CSV, checks them against current EU e-invoicing and public-procurement readiness rules, and exports a practical compliance checklist.

Objective for this build

Build a single-window macOS AppKit app in Swift that accepts a folder of mixed invoice samples, extracts basic invoice metadata, highlights missing or risky fields, fetches and caches official public e-invoicing guidance pages for context, and exports a dated readiness report. The smallest useful artifact is a working .dmg-ready desktop app with drag-and-drop import, progress feedback, error states, and text export.
