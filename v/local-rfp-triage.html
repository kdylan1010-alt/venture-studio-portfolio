The opportunity

Local RFP/Bid Triage Assistant for small contractors: target users are small government-contracting and B2B bid teams that lose time and money to missed deadlines, mandatory attachments, and compliance mistakes. Value proposition: a native macOS desktop app that locally reads an RFP and turns it into a concrete submission checklist, reducing missed requirements without any cloud dependency.

Objective for this build

Build the smallest macOS desktop artifact that proves the thesis: a one-window Swift app that loads a sample RFP text or PDF, extracts due date and mandatory checklist items, and shows them in a local report view.

**MARKET SIZE**
This is a niche B2B workflow tool for small contractors and bid teams that regularly handle formal RFPs, RFQs, or procurement packages. My bottom-up estimate is:

- ESTIMATE 25% of those are small enough to lack a heavyweight proposal ops stack, which leaves ESTIMATE 7,500 organizations.

- ESTIMATE 2 to 3 likely users per organization gives ESTIMATE 3,000 to 6,750 potential seats.
- If expanded beyond the Mac wedge to cross-platform later, the reachable buyer pool could plausibly be ESTIMATE 3x to 5x larger.

This is inference, not a checked source. The practical interpretation: the initial market is real but narrow, and it is better suited to a focused SMB utility than a broad platform bet.
