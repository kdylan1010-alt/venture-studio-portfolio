The opportunity

subscription-cancel-proof-packet: a native macOS desktop app for consumers and advocates who need to prove a subscription cancellation. Pain point: vendors hide confirmations, keep billing, or make the cancellation trail hard to reconstruct. Value proposition: a local app that assembles screenshots, receipts, call notes, and a structured timeline into a clean cancellation-proof packet for disputes or chargebacks.

Objective for this build

Build the smallest offline Swift artifact that reads a clean and a crafted local fixture folder, evaluates whether a cancellation proof packet is complete, and prints an evidence report that demonstrates the thesis without any network or third-party dependency.
