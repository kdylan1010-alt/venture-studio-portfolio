The opportunity

Accessibility Evidence Pack for SaaS teams, agencies, and digital product owners shipping into the EU: a native macOS app that checks staging sites, local HTML, screenshots, and PDFs for accessibility defects, then turns the findings into a shareable evidence packet that helps teams prove readiness and prioritize remediation.

Objective for this build

Build a native macOS AppKit tool in Swift that accepts a URL, local HTML bundle, screenshot set, or PDF, renders content with WKWebView and PDFKit, runs a focused accessibility audit for headings, alt text, form labels, landmark structure, and contrast heuristics, then exports a prioritized PDF/CSV evidence packet while persisting the last target and user settings.
