The opportunity

AI Content Disclosure Desk for EU-facing agencies, publishers, and solo creators who now have to label synthetic content under the AI Act. It reduces the manual burden of spotting likely AI-generated assets, generating required disclosures, and assembling audit-ready provenance packets.

Objective for this build

Build a native macOS app that imports a folder of images, PDFs, and text files, applies OCR and text heuristics to flag likely AI-generated or altered assets, attaches disclosure labels, and exports a compliance summary plus per-file audit log.
