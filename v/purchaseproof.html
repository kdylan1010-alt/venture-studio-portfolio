The opportunity

PurchaseProof, a native macOS purchase-record recovery and warranty assistant for ordinary households, freelancers, and small-shop owners. The painful problem is that proof of purchase is fragmented across scans, screenshots, PDFs, and exported messages, making returns, reimbursements, repairs, insurance claims, and warranty use unnecessarily difficult. Its value proposition is reliable on-device extraction from messy evidence into a searchable library that surfaces deadlines and exports useful records without uploading private documents.

Objective for this build

Build a shippable native macOS PurchaseProof application and distributable .dmg using swiftc and Apple system frameworks alone. The graphical app must accept drag-and-drop PDFs, images, screenshots, and text/email-export files; process batches with visible progress; use PDFKit and Vision OCR; extract and let users correct merchant, date, items, amount, serial number, return deadline, and warranty clues; retain source-to-field provenance; detect likely duplicates; persist its library and settings across launches; provide search, filters, purchase detail, and useful error recovery; and export selected records to CSV plus return/warranty dates to an .ics calendar. Verification must exercise mixed messy fixtures, relaunch persistence, correction flow, duplicate handling, exports, application launch, and DMG contents.
