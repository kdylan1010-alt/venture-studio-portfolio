The opportunity

LumaLoop is a native macOS live-wallpaper engine for people who want their Mac to feel personal and alive. Users install their own videos, photos, and animations as polished backgrounds across every connected display, with adaptive playback that respects screen geometry, appearance, battery state, Low Power Mode, Reduce Motion, Spaces, and visibility.

Objective for this build

Continue LumaLoop in place and add the day-3 credits foundation: a generous monthly allowance that refills automatically, a clear balance and renewal date, persisted transaction history, graceful exhausted-credit messaging, and the entitlement structure needed for a future paid tier. Credits may cover optional premium processing or enhancements only; selecting, installing, and playing the user's wallpapers must remain unlimited. Preserve the complete native wallpaper experience, multi-display adaptation, energy-aware playback, useful progress and errors, persistent settings, and every required visible Quit path.

Market and user research

## 1. MARKET SIZE

All figures below are **ESTIMATES**, derived bottom-up because no validated market dataset or usable `RESEARCH_SOURCES.md` competition table was available.

- **ESTIMATE:** 100 million active Mac users worldwide.
- **ESTIMATE:** 15% actively customize their desktop beyond the defaults: 15 million users.
- **ESTIMATE:** 20% of those users are interested in animated or personal-media wallpapers: 3 million plausible users.
- **ESTIMATE:** 20% would pay for a reliable native utility: **600,000 potential buyers**.
- At an **ESTIMATED $14.99 one-time price**, the theoretical category revenue is approximately **$9 million gross** over a product lifetime.
- A more realistic serviceable market—English-speaking users on supported modern Macs—is **ESTIMATED at 400,000 buyers**, or roughly **$6 million gross** at $14.99.
- Capturing an **ESTIMATED 1%** of that serviceable market would produce 4,000 customers and about **$60,000 gross revenue** before store fees, taxes, support, and development costs.
- Capturing an **ESTIMATED 5%** would produce 20,000 customers and approximately **$300,000 gross revenue**.

The broad audience is large, but willingness to pay for desktop personalization is the limiting step. LumaLoop is more plausibly a durable small software business than a venture-scale company unless premium media transformations create meaningful recurring revenue.
